About Me
Experience: 3+ years in UX/UI Design
Location: Lewisville, Texas
Summary: I'm a Product Designer at CVS Health designing enterprise and healthcare platforms. I turn complex systems into intuitive experiences. My work spans the full design cycle—from understanding user needs and stakeholder requirements to shipping implementation-ready designs in Figma. I thrive in ambiguous spaces, move fast through iteration, and collaborate closely with product and engineering teams.
What I do: Design intuitive interfaces for enterprise platforms and healthcare workflows Create user flows and information architecture for complex data systems Partner with cross-functional teams to ship designs in Agile environments
Why it matters: I studied Psychology and Art, which shaped how I approach design as both empathy and craft. Every product I touch gets clearer, more usable, and better aligned with what users actually need. I'm most energized by solving hard UX problems—the kind that require balancing business goals, technical constraints, and real user needs. That's where good design lives.

Projects
> CVS Caremark - myPBM
Project: - Clinical Requirements (CLR)
Role: Lead UX/UI Designer
Year: 2022 - 2024
Modernized complex clinical workflow for pharmacy benefit management platform. Working alongside pharmacists, external clients, and business managers to gather business requirements. Worked closely with engineers, scrum teams, and product managers to ship features in an Agile environment.
Led user research, wireframing, and usability testing. Helped establish design system and UX best practices across the product team. My work on this project has improved user satisfaction and efficiency for pharmacy staff and healthcare providers. Saving time and reducing errors in critical healthcare workflows has a real impact on patient care.
> CVS Health - Aetna Marketing
Project: Member Growth Engine (MGE)
Role: UX/UI Designer
Year: 2024 - 2025
Helped design and launch new marketing planning tool for Aetna marketing and data science teams. Transformed manual spreadsheet processes into an intuitive web application. Collaborated with data scientists to visualize complex marketing data and KPIs. Worked closely with engineers to implement designs.
Worked with 3-4 product owners and data scientists to gather requirements and iterate on designs. My work on this project has streamlined marketing planning processes and improved data visibility for Aetna's marketing teams, enabling them to make more informed decisions and optimize their campaigns. Saving upwards of 20+ hours per week in manual work has a significant impact on team productivity and campaign effectiveness.
> CVS Health - Analytics & Behavior Change (A&BC)
Project: Enterprise Data Portal (EDP)
Role: UX/UI Designer
Year: 2025 - 2026
Helped design new features and maintain design standards for EDP. EDP is a data portal tool where application teams, developers, business stakeholders, and governance can access datasets, schemas from various authors and teams across the enterprise. Worked closely with data engineers and product managers to understand complex data structures and design intuitive interfaces for data access request and marketplace patterns.
During this time was able to access AI tools like Figma Make, Cursor, Copilot, and Claude Code to experiment locally on how AI can help with design tasks. I was able to use these tools to speed up my design process and iterate faster on ideas.
